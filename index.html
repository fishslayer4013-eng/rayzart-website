Before you tow

A safer rental starts with the right setup.

Rayzart confirms the tow vehicle and connection before releasing a trailer. Requirements vary by trailer and load.

✓

Correct hitch
Deck trailers require a 2-5/16-inch ball.

✓

Brake controller
A working controller is required for the electric trailer brakes.

✓

Adequate tow rating
Your vehicle must be rated for the trailer and expected load.

✓

Secure the load
Proper straps, chains and binders are required.

✓

Refundable deposit
A $200 damage deposit commonly applies and is confirmed before pickup.

✓

Plan ahead
At least 24 hours’ notice is appreciated when possible.

Common questions

Know before you reserve.

How do I reserve a trailer?

Submit the availability request or call/text Rayzart. Your dates are confirmed after the trailer, tow vehicle and pickup or delivery details are verified.

Are dump fees included?

No. Dump and disposal fees are separate unless a written quote specifically includes them.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery may be available for an additional charge depending on the trailer, location and schedule.

Can I rent straps, chains or binders?

Safety and securement equipment may be available for a small additional fee. Ask about your load when reserving.

What if I am not sure which trailer I need?

Describe the vehicle, equipment, material or cleanup project. Rayzart will help determine whether a deck trailer, dump trailer or hauling service is the better fit.
